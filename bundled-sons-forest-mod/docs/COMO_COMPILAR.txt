1) Copie LivePlaySonsForestBridge.cs e LivePlaySonsForestBridge.csproj para:
   C:\Users\diogo\OneDrive\Desktop\LivePlaySonsForestBridgeBuild

2) No PowerShell dentro dessa pasta, rode:
   dotnet clean
   dotnet build -c Release

3) Copie a DLL gerada de:
   bin\Release\net6.0\LivePlaySonsForestBridge.dll

4) Para teste direto no jogo, coloque em:
   G:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Sons Of The Forest\BepInEx\plugins\LivePlaySonsForestBridge.dll

5) Para o instalador do LivePlay copiar automaticamente no futuro, coloque também em:
   C:\Users\diogo\OneDrive\Desktop\LivePlays\public\bundled-sons-forest-mod\plugins\LivePlaySonsForestBridge.dll

6) Abra o jogo e confira no BepInEx\LogOutput.log se aparece:
   LivePlay Sons Forest Bridge ativo na porta 35952
